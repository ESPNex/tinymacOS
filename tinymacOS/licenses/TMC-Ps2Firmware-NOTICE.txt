tinymacOS derived PS/2 firmware driver

TmcPs2KeyboardDxe.efi is a declared derivative of the Ps2KeyboardDxe
firmware driver included by OpenCore, not an official unmodified OpenCore
binary. OpenCore itself and the other vendored OpenCore drivers are unchanged.

The optional raw set-1 protocol mirrors bytes already read by the firmware
keyboard driver. It preserves standard UEFI text input and lets Recovery
apply its sole Italian physical layout without guessing a firmware Unicode
layout. It does not grant Recovery ownership of controller ports.
