Binary assets of the historical 2.4.3 source mirror are losslessly archived in historical-assets.tar.xz to reserve snapshot capacity for Tramontana. All original paths relative to sources are preserved inside the archive and SHA-256 verified before removing the redundant expanded copies. Extract to an external scratch directory with tar -xJf historical-assets.tar.xz -C /var/tmp/reconstruction. Canonical tinymacOS/assets is unaffected.
